## Competitive Landscape (Tabuk Salons)

- Multiple salons in Tabuk compete for the same demographic
- Most rely on Instagram and walk-ins without dedicated web presence
- Opportunity to differentiate with a premium, editorial-style landing page
- Key differentiator: BEAUTIQUE offers nail, hair, spa, AND gym under one roof

## Gap Analysis

- No standalone website or landing page currently live
- Linktree is functional but generic; does not convey brand identity
- Instagram is strong but cannot replace a proper web presence for search visibility
- A dedicated landing page would improve discoverability, credibility, and booking conversion

## Design Direction

- Modern, chic, monochrome palette with a single accent color
- Editorial / fashion-magazine aesthetic to stand out from competitors
- Bilingual (Arabic / English) to serve full Tabuk demographic
- Clean service archetype: clarity, elegance, minimal ornamentation
