# Research: Fares' Steakhouse - Tabuk, Saudi Arabia

## Market Context

### Tabuk Dining Landscape
- Tabuk is a rapidly developing city in northwestern Saudi Arabia with a growing affluent population
- The city has seen significant investment under Vision 2030, attracting high-net-worth residents
- Premium dining options remain limited compared to Riyadh/Jeddah, creating a strong opportunity
- Al-Salam district is a well-established residential area with upscale clientele

### Competitive Analysis
- Few dedicated premium steakhouses exist in Tabuk; most steak offerings are within hotel restaurants or casual dining
- Fares' Steakhouse occupies a niche as an independent fine-dining steakhouse
- Rating of 4.2/5 with 791 reviews indicates established reputation and consistent traffic
- High price positioning filters for the target VIP demographic

### Target Audience Profile
- Male-dominated clientele (Saudi steakhouse culture), ages 28-55
- Business professionals, military officers (Tabuk is a major military city), entrepreneurs
- High disposable income, accustomed to premium experiences
- Value exclusivity, privacy, and personal service
- Many have traveled internationally and benchmark against global steakhouse brands

## Industry Research: Premium Steakhouse Digital Presence

### Global Steakhouse Branding Patterns
- Dark color palettes dominate (black, deep brown, burgundy, charcoal)
- Gold or copper accents signal luxury without ostentation
- Photography is always high-contrast, dramatic lighting on meat
- Typography leans serif for headings (authority, tradition) and clean sans-serif for body
- Menus always feature cut diagrams and doneness guides

### Steak Cut Education (Key Content)
- Ribeye: Most marbled, richest flavor, forgiving at various temperatures
- Tenderloin/Filet Mignon: Most tender, lean, butter-soft texture
- New York Strip: Balance of tenderness and flavor, firm bite
- T-Bone: Best of both worlds (strip + tenderloin)
- Tomahawk: Dramatic presentation, bone-in ribeye, signature showpiece

### Doneness Levels (Universal Standard)
1. Blue Rare - Cool red center, barely seared
2. Rare - Cool-to-warm red center, soft
3. Medium Rare - Warm red center, ideal for most premium cuts
4. Medium - Warm pink center, firmer texture
5. Medium Well - Slight pink, mostly cooked through
6. Well Done - No pink, fully cooked

### VIP Dining Trends in Saudi Arabia
- Private dining rooms are essential for high-value customers
- WhatsApp is the primary reservation channel (not web forms)
- Personal relationships with staff/management matter greatly
- Presentation and "Instagram moments" drive word-of-mouth
- Wine is not served (Saudi Arabia); focus on premium non-alcoholic beverages, specialty teas, Arabic coffee

**Important Note:** Saudi Arabia prohibits alcohol. "Wine pairing" section should be reframed as "Beverage Pairing" featuring premium non-alcoholic alternatives, artisan juices, and specialty drinks.
