## Market Context

### Tabuk Dining Landscape
Tabuk is a growing city in northwestern Saudi Arabia with an expanding middle and upper class. The dining scene is evolving rapidly, with increasing demand for international cuisine experiences that go beyond traditional fast-casual offerings. Japanese cuisine occupies a premium niche in the Saudi market, associated with sophistication, quality ingredients, and artisanal craft.

### Japanese Cuisine in Saudi Arabia
The Japanese food segment in Saudi Arabia has experienced significant growth, driven by:
- Rising cosmopolitan tastes among younger Saudi demographics
- Influence of travel to Japan and East Asian destinations
- Social media-driven food culture emphasizing visual presentation
- Vision 2030 lifestyle diversification creating appetite for global experiences

### Competitive Position
Hikari operates in a relatively underserved niche within Tabuk. While Riyadh and Jeddah have numerous Japanese dining options, Tabuk has limited high-end Japanese offerings. This positions Hikari as a destination restaurant rather than one of many competitors.
