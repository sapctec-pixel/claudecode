# Shahad Sweet - Research Notes

## Business Overview
- **Name (AR):** حلويات شهد
- **Name (EN):** Shahad Sweet
- **Category:** Premium bakery and sweets shop
- **City:** Tabuk, Saudi Arabia
- **Primary District:** Tabuk Al-Jadidah (تبوك الجديدة)

## Branches
Shahad Sweet operates multiple branches across the Tabuk region:
1. **Al Olya (العليا)** - Main/flagship branch
2. **Al Rawda (الروضة)**
3. **Al Dakhl (الدخل)**
4. **Al Hamra (الحمراء)**
5. **Al Murooj (المروج)**
6. **Al Masif (المصيف)**
7. **Tabuk Al-Jadidah (تبوك الجديدة)**

This wide branch coverage indicates a well-established, trusted brand across Tabuk.

## Social Media Presence
- **Instagram:** @shahad.sweet0 - 3,796 followers. Active posting of products, cakes, and seasonal offerings.
- **Facebook:** Active page with customer engagement
- **Snapchat:** Used for behind-the-scenes and daily specials
- **TikTok:** Growing presence with product showcases
- **HungerStation:** Listed for delivery orders

## Product Lines
- Custom celebration cakes (birthdays, weddings, graduations)
- Traditional Arabic sweets (baklava, kunafa, basbousa)
- Seasonal and Ramadan specials
- Event platters and corporate gifting
- Daily fresh pastries and desserts

## Digital Presence Assessment
- **Website:** None found. No existing web storefront.
- **Online Ordering:** Currently relies on HungerStation and Instagram DMs for orders
- **Gap:** Strong social following but no centralized e-catalog or pre-ordering system

## Competitive Position
- One of the largest multi-branch sweet shops in Tabuk
- Strong local brand recognition
- Customer base spans traditional sweets enthusiasts and modern cake buyers
- Opportunity to formalize online ordering and event booking through a dedicated landing page

## Key Opportunities
1. E-catalog showcasing full product range
2. Pre-ordering system for cakes and custom orders
3. Special occasion booking (weddings, corporate events)
4. Branch directory with locations and hours
5. Centralized contact point instead of scattered social DMs
