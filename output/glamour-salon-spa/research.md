# Research: Glamour Salon & Spa - Tabuk

## Business Overview

- **Name:** Glamour Salon & Spa
- **Category:** Salon / Spa
- **Location:** Murooj Al-Amir District (مروج الأمير), Tabuk, Saudi Arabia
- **Phone:** 0541080812
- **Current Online Presence:** None identified

## Market Context

### Tabuk Beauty Market
- Tabuk is a growing city in northwestern Saudi Arabia with an expanding consumer market.
- Beauty and personal care services are in high demand, especially among women aged 18-45.
- The Murooj Al-Amir district is a residential area with steady foot traffic and local clientele.
- Competition includes local salons and national chains; few have professional digital presences.

### Target Audience
- Women aged 18-50 in Tabuk and surrounding areas.
- Brides-to-be and bridal parties seeking comprehensive packages.
- Professionals seeking regular grooming and maintenance services.
- Residents of Murooj Al-Amir and neighboring districts.

### Competitive Landscape
- Most salons in Tabuk rely on social media (Instagram, Snapchat) rather than dedicated websites.
- Price transparency is rare; most competitors require direct inquiry for pricing.
- A professional landing page with clear pricing would be a significant competitive advantage.
- Bilingual content (Arabic/English) serves the diverse population of Tabuk.

## Service Categories Identified

1. **Hair Services** - Cuts, coloring, treatments, keratin, blowouts
2. **Skin Care** - Facials, peels, dermabrasion, hydrafacial
3. **Nail Services** - Manicure, pedicure, gel, acrylic, nail art
4. **Spa & Body** - Massage, Moroccan bath, body wraps, scrubs
5. **Bridal Packages** - Complete bridal preparation, trials, group packages

## Key Findings

- Price transparency is the top request from potential customers in the region.
- WhatsApp is the dominant booking channel; a direct CTA link is essential.
- Before/after showcases build trust in beauty services.
- Testimonials from real clients drive conversion in this segment.
- Arabic-first design with English toggle serves the local demographic best.

## Recommendations

- Build a clean, price-transparent landing page.
- Emphasize current offers and seasonal promotions prominently.
- Include WhatsApp booking integration as the primary CTA.
- Showcase team expertise to build credibility.
- Use a glamorous but professional aesthetic -- not overly zen/spa or overly flashy.
