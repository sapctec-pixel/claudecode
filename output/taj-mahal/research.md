# Taj Mahal Restaurant - Research

## Business Profile

- **Name:** Taj Mahal Restaurant (مطعم تاج محل)
- **Location:** Murooj Al-Amir (مروج الأمير), Tabuk, Saudi Arabia
- **Category:** Independent Indian Restaurant
- **Scale:** One of the largest Indian restaurants in Tabuk
- **Rating:** 4.0/5 from 2,957 reviews
- **Status:** Currently Open

## Market Context

### Tabuk Dining Scene
- Tabuk is a major city in northwestern Saudi Arabia with a growing food scene
- Indian cuisine is highly popular across Saudi Arabia, with a large South Asian expatriate community
- Competition includes smaller Indian restaurants and chain establishments
- The "largest Indian restaurant" positioning gives Taj Mahal a significant advantage for group dining and events

### Target Audience
1. **Saudi families** seeking authentic Indian dining experiences for large gatherings
2. **South Asian expatriates** looking for familiar, high-quality Indian food
3. **Young professionals** interested in convenient ordering and spice-level clarity
4. **Corporate groups** needing space for business dining

## Business Needs Analysis

### Primary Pain Points
1. **Order automation:** Current reliance on waiters for ordering creates bottlenecks, especially during peak hours in a large restaurant
2. **Spice level confusion:** Non-Indian diners unfamiliar with dishes need clear guidance on ingredients and heat levels
3. **Waiter dependency:** Large restaurant floor means slow service if understaffed

### Proposed Solutions
1. **WhatsApp ordering integration:** Allow customers to browse menu, understand dishes, and place orders via WhatsApp
2. **Spice level indicators:** Visual system (mild/medium/hot) on every menu item
3. **Ingredient transparency:** Brief descriptions of key ingredients for each dish category
4. **Self-service menu browsing:** Detailed digital menu reduces need for waiter explanations

## Competitive Differentiation

### vs. Biryani House (sister project)
- **Scale:** Taj Mahal is grander, more formal
- **Design:** More ornamental, richer color palette (jewel tones vs. warm spice tones)
- **Layout:** More generous spacing, premium feel
- **Sections:** Includes group dining packages, more elaborate tradition section
- **Typography:** More formal, serif accents for headings

### vs. Generic Indian Restaurants
- Grand heritage storytelling
- Comprehensive spice guide section
- Group dining focus (leveraging the large space)
- Premium visual presentation matching the restaurant's scale

## Content Strategy

### Tone of Voice
- **Arabic:** Formal yet warm, using classical Arabic food terminology
- **English:** Refined, inviting, heritage-focused
- Emphasize tradition, craftsmanship, and generosity
- No emoji - let the rich design elements convey warmth

### Visual Direction
- Deep royal purple as primary brand color (royalty, richness)
- Saffron gold accents (India's signature spice color)
- Ruby red highlights (warmth, spice, celebration)
- Cream backgrounds (clean, warm, inviting)
- Ornamental CSS borders inspired by Indian architectural patterns
- Generous whitespace to convey premium quality
