# Fakhama Tabuk - Research Report

## Business Overview

**Name:** Fakhama Tabuk (فخامة تبوك)
**Category:** Furniture - Arab Councils (Majlis) and Interior Detailing
**Location:** Tabuk, Saudi Arabia
**Current Digital Presence:** TikTok only

## Market Context

### Tabuk Furniture Market
- Tabuk is a growing city in northwestern Saudi Arabia with expanding residential development
- Villa construction is increasing as part of Saudi Vision 2030 urban development
- High-net-worth residents seek bespoke Arab council (majlis) furnishing for their villas
- Limited number of premium, dedicated majlis furniture makers in the region
- Most competitors rely on word-of-mouth and social media only

### Target Audience
- Villa owners in Tabuk and surrounding areas
- High-net-worth individuals furnishing new properties
- Clients seeking traditional Arab council designs with modern luxury finishes
- Real estate developers furnishing model villas
- Government and corporate clients needing VIP reception rooms

### Competitive Landscape
- Local competitors primarily use Instagram and TikTok for marketing
- Very few have dedicated websites or professional digital portfolios
- National competitors (Riyadh, Jeddah) have more sophisticated online presence
- Opportunity to differentiate through a premium web portfolio

## Product Categories

1. **Majlis (Arab Councils)** - Traditional seating arrangements, floor-level and raised
2. **Reception Halls** - Formal guest reception furniture and detailing
3. **Dining Rooms** - Luxury dining sets with Arabic design elements
4. **Bedrooms** - Master bedroom suites with bespoke headboards and wardrobes

## Key Selling Points
- Custom, handcrafted furniture tailored to each client's space
- Premium materials: solid wood, premium fabrics, brushed metals
- End-to-end service from consultation to installation
- Deep understanding of traditional Arab design aesthetics
- Attention to detail in carving, upholstery, and finishing

## Digital Strategy Needs
- Professional portfolio showcasing completed projects
- Category-based gallery for easy browsing
- WhatsApp integration for consultation requests
- Bilingual content (Arabic primary, English secondary)
- Mobile-first design for social media traffic conversion
- Trust signals: testimonials, process transparency, material quality

## Content Strategy
- Hero imagery should evoke warmth, sophistication, and grandeur
- Portfolio should be the centerpiece of the page
- Process section builds trust by showing professionalism
- Materials section highlights quality and craftsmanship
- Testimonials from satisfied villa owners add social proof
